# Notices and credits

## HM Treasury Green Book (UK)

Choosewell summarises and paraphrases guidance from HM Treasury's *The Green Book: appraisal and evaluation in central government* (2026 edition) and its supplementary guidance, including *Use of Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis in options appraisal of economic cases* (2024) and *Accounting for the effects of climate change*. See `references/rulebooks/green-book.md`.

Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/

Choosewell is not endorsed by HM Treasury. For any business case, check the current Green Book on gov.uk: the summary here may be out of date.

## US federal guidance

The US federal rulebook (`references/rulebooks/us-federal.md`) summarises OMB Circulars A-94 and A-4 and the GAO *Cost Estimating and Assessment Guide* (GAO-16-22). Works of the US government are not subject to copyright in the United States. Choosewell is not endorsed by OMB or GAO.

## Other methods

Other frameworks are credited where they're used and listed with their sources in `references/presenting/sources-of-methods.md`. Choosewell describes these methods in its own words and doesn't reproduce their texts.

## Worked examples

The worked examples are illustrative composites with details changed, or are invented. They don't describe any real organisation's decision, and their numbers are illustrations, not benchmarks.
